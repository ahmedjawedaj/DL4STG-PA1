\section*{Generative AI use}
I used Claude (Anthropic) as a coding and writing assistant, with occasional review passes
from OpenAI Codex, in the way the course policy allows. It helped with the implementation
cells in the Task 1 harness, the Task 2 pipeline and comparison scripts, first drafts of
report text and tables from the saved results, and a check of the final files against the
handout requirements (168 values in order, declared $P$ and $E$ matching the saved runs).

\textbf{Prompt summaries, assistance and follow-up.}
The questions below are retrospective paraphrases of the AI-assisted workflow, not
verbatim chat excerpts. They summarise the reasoning behind the experiments rather
than reproduce the full conversation.
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Understanding Autoformer:} How do decomposition and AutoCorrelation
        work, and how can the supplied future inputs help with the 168-step forecast?
        The assistance explained the model components and helped with implementation.
        The resulting code was checked against the required architecture and notebook tests.
  \item \textbf{Interpreting submission 1:} Why did historical RMSE of 69.34 become
        81.56 on the leaderboard? Could this be an export error or uneven performance
        across weeks? The audit reproduced the forecasts from saved weights and compared
        weekly errors. The report was corrected to distinguish historical performance
        from an unseen-week result; the earlier expectation of first place was not justified.
  \item \textbf{Choosing the next experiments:} Would more parameters or epochs help,
        or were the large errors pointing to another limitation? The assistance proposed
        capacity, refit and feature variants, and examined historical peak errors.
        Small or inconsistent gains were not treated as fixes. A second chronological
        fold and paired weekly comparisons were added to strengthen the evidence.
  \item \textbf{Learning from submission 2:} Why did a nonlinear input embedding
        improve both historical folds but not the submitted week? The comparisons showed
        20 of 33 difficult-period weeks improved, while leaderboard RMSE rose to 81.93.
        This negative result was retained in the report rather than presented as a
        successful change; historical gains were not taken as a guarantee.
  \item \textbf{Reconsidering the input path:} Could a simpler model use the future
        inputs more effectively, and what would that suggest for Autoformer?
        Diagnostic MLP and boosting comparisons motivated a direct covariate head.
        These comparators were not submitted. The report treats suppression of input
        information as a hypothesis, not an established cause.
  \item \textbf{Selecting submission 3:} Does the revised approach improve across
        periods and seeds, and which saved ensemble is justified? The direct-head
        configuration, together with the removal of anchoring and a training-schedule change, improved
        24 of 33 difficult-period weeks. Seeds 2, 3 and 4 were selected on the
        early-stopping set. The export and resource counts were checked
        ($P=53\,670$, $E=20$); submission 3 achieved RMSE 62.95.
        The report attributes the gain to the combined configuration, not the head alone.
\end{enumerate}

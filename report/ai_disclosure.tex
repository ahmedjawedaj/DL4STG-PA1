\section*{Generative AI use}
I used Claude (Anthropic) for coding and report drafts, and OpenAI Codex for reviewing
the implementation, comparing variants and checking forecast export. Assistance
included the Task 1 implementation cells, the Task 2 pipeline and draft explanations
based on saved results.

The examples below are \textbf{excerpts from my actual messages to Codex}, in
chronological order. Original wording is retained with line breaks removed.
Outputs are summarised; these examples cover the review and development stages,
rather than the original implementation requests to Claude.

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Reviewing the existing solution.}

        \textbf{Prompt excerpt:} \textit{claude did some work please review and ensure
        its fulfilling the assignment requirements from a student}

        \textbf{Output:} A review of the notebook, Autoformer mechanisms, required
        ablation and submission files. It confirmed the implemented checks and
        identified missing report fields and an incomplete reproduction command.

        \textbf{Edits/checks:} Missing fields were filled, reproduction instructions
        were aligned with the saved model settings, and explanations were shortened
        while keeping the required evidence.

  \item \textbf{Preserving candidates and comparing variants.}

        \textbf{Prompt excerpts:} \textit{okay save current settings/weights whatever
        so we can use later for submissions}; \textit{and keep saving outputs for
        every variant}; \textit{i would encourage you to try with better params and
        epochs and try best configs ti get us best output}

        \textbf{Output:} Saved weights, predictions and settings for earlier
        candidates; a wider-model trial; and two- and three-seed ensemble comparisons.

        \textbf{Edits/checks:} The wider trial was stopped when early-stopping results
        remained worse. The compact three-seed anchored candidate was selected for
        attempt 1 from historical comparisons. Ensemble parameter and epoch counts
        were summed, and earlier candidates were retained.

  \item \textbf{Investigating the first leaderboard result.}

        \textbf{Prompt excerpt:} \textit{you said we will be on top no we are on 4th
        review this now and fix}

        \textbf{Output:} The earlier rank prediction was corrected. An audit reproduced
        the forecasts from saved weights and checked scaling and ordering. Anchored
        and unanchored refits were compared on historical blocks; the best new mixture
        improved pooled RMSE by only 0.37, with uncertain benefit across weeks.

        \textbf{Edits/checks:} Attempt 1 was preserved, and the small refit gain was
        not presented as a reliable fix or a future leaderboard score. Later
        configurations were assessed on historical folds, as described in Task 2.
\end{enumerate}

Report edits and checks were also AI-assisted. Tables were checked against saved
CSV/JSON records, and the final export was checked for 168 ordered values with
$P=53\,670$ and $E=20$. The report states the reused-validation and final-model
limitations. I remain responsible for the submitted code, results and explanations.
